Summary AissaGames respects your privacy. Kharbga is free to play and designed to collect as little data as possible. Core account, save, and leaderboard features are provided by Unity Gaming Services. You can delete your account and data at any time. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Developer information

This game and website are operated by AissaGames ("we", "us", "our"), an independent game developer and the data controller responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

  • Developer: AissaGames
  • Product: Kharbga (mobile game) and this website, aissagames.com
  • Contact email: aissamak121991@gmail.com

2. Scope & consent

This Privacy Policy applies to the Kharbga application on all platforms where it is distributed, and to this website. By downloading, accessing, or using Kharbga or this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy. Where required by law, we ask for your consent before processing certain data, and you may withdraw that consent at any time.

4. Unity Gaming Services

Kharbga is built with Unity and uses Unity Gaming Services (UGS) from Unity Technologies to provide core online features. Depending on the features you use, the following services may process your data:

  • Unity Authentication & Unity Player Accounts — creates and manages your player identity, including guest, Google, and email sign-in and account linking.
  • Unity Cloud Save — securely stores your game progress and settings so they follow you across devices.
  • Unity Economy — manages in-game virtual currency and items associated with your account.
  • Unity Leaderboards — stores your scores and ranking to enable competition.
  • Unity Cloud Code — runs trusted server-side game logic (for example, validating results).
  • Unity Analytics — provides aggregated and event-level insight into how the game is played.

Unity acts as our data processor / service provider for these features and processes data on our behalf under its own terms and privacy practices. You can review Unity's privacy policy at unity.com/legal. Your Unity Player Account is governed by Unity's account terms in addition to ours.

5. Crash reporting

To keep Kharbga stable, we collect crash and diagnostic reports when the game encounters an error. These reports may include the device model, operating system version, app version, and technical details about the crash. They are used solely to identify and fix problems and are not used to advertise to you.

6. Future advertising

Kharbga currently ships with a clean, ad-light design and does not display third-party ads. Advertising — including banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads served through Google AdMob — may become available in future updates. This feature is not currently enabled.

If advertising is introduced, we will update this policy beforehand and, where required, request your consent. Any advertising partners and the data they process would be disclosed here, you would be able to choose non-personalised ads where the platform supports it, and we would never knowingly serve personalised advertising to children.

7. Future purchases & live features

Kharbga is currently free to play with no purchases. In-app purchases, subscriptions, rewarded offers, promotional (promo) codes, a season pass, live events, and tournaments may become available in future updates. These features are not currently enabled.

If paid features are introduced, payment is handled entirely by the relevant platform store (such as Google Play or the App Store); we do not receive or store your full payment card details. We may receive a confirmation of purchase and a transaction identifier to deliver your item and support refunds. Where any future live feature processes additional data, this policy will be updated to describe it before the feature is enabled. Purchase terms are described in our Terms of Service.

8. Website cookies

This website is intentionally lightweight. It does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. We use only a small amount of local browser storage to remember your preferences — specifically your chosen light or dark theme. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us. The Kharbga game itself does not use browser cookies.

9. Website analytics

We do not run invasive third-party analytics on this website. If we ever introduce privacy-respecting, aggregate website analytics, this section will be updated to describe the provider and the data involved, and to honour your choices.

10. How we use data

  • To create and authenticate your player account and keep it secure.
  • To save and synchronise your progress across devices.
  • To operate leaderboards, virtual currency, and other game features.
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve performance and gameplay.
  • To respond to your support requests and communicate important changes.
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, cheating, and abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except the service providers described in this policy or where required by law.

11. Legal bases for processing

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases: performance of a contract (to provide the game and its features you request); legitimate interests (to secure, maintain, and improve the game, balanced against your rights); consent (for optional features such as any future advertising, where required); and legal obligation (to comply with applicable law).

12. Security

We and our service providers use technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit, access controls, and trusted infrastructure provided by Unity Gaming Services. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to promptly address any issues we discover.

13. Data retention

We retain account and game data for as long as your account remains active, so that your progress is available to you. Diagnostic and analytics data are retained for a limited period necessary to maintain and improve the game. When you delete your account, associated personal data is deleted or anonymised as described below, except where we are required to keep certain records to comply with legal obligations.

14. Deletion & account removal

You can delete your Unity Player Account and its associated data at any time. Deleting your account permanently removes your Cloud Save data, leaderboard entries, and Economy balances, and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account Follow the steps on our Delete Account page, or go directly to Unity's deletion portal at player-account.unity.com/delete-account. You may also email us and we will assist you.

15. Children's privacy

Kharbga is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without appropriate consent. If we become aware that we have collected such data without the necessary consent, we will delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take action. Any future advertising will be configured to avoid personalised ads for users identified as children.

16. International transfers

Because we use cloud infrastructure and Unity Gaming Services, your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including outside the European Economic Area. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we and our providers rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms to protect your information.

17. GDPR rights (EU / UK)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to: access your personal data; rectify inaccurate data; erase your data; restrict or object to processing; data portability; and withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at aissamak121991@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframes required by law.

18. CCPA rights (California)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used, to request deletion of your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising your rights. We do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act. To exercise your rights, contact us at aissamak121991@gmail.com.

19. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the game, our practices, or the law. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and version number above and, where appropriate, notify you in the game or on this website. Your continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
