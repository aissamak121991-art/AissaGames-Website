MINDLOCK — Color Code Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026 · Last updated: October 14, 2026

Language: English العربية Français

The short version. MINDLOCK is a single-player code-breaking puzzle game that runs offline. Your progress, coins, treasures, daily-challenge record and settings are saved only on your device — we have no servers and no accounts, so we never receive them. The game shows advertisements through Unity LevelPlay, an advertising mediation service from Unity Technologies, with Unity Ads as an advertising network. That advertising software does process some information about your device in order to deliver those ads. This policy explains exactly which is which.

At a glance

  • No account, no sign-in, no profile.
  • No name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or GPS location.
  • No analytics and no crash-reporting service in the game.
  • Game progress stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.
  • Advertising is served through Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads; their software processes the advertising data described in section 4.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your choice before any advertisement is requested, and you can change it later in Settings.
  • There are no in-app purchases.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers the MINDLOCK mobile game only. It does not cover Google Play, Unity's advertising services, or any other website or service you may reach from the game — those are governed by their own policies, linked in section 15.

We describe what the game actually does. Where something is done by a third party's software inside the app rather than by us, we say so plainly instead of blurring the two.

3. Information stored on your device

The game saves a small record in the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded, and we never receive it. It contains no personal information:

  • Your progress — which levels and worlds you have completed and the stars earned on each.
  • Your in-game items — coins you have earned, your power-ups (Reveal, Eliminate, Extra attempts) and the treasures you have collected from vaults.
  • Your daily-challenge record — the guesses of today's puzzle (so a reopened game resumes it), games played, wins, current and best streak, and how many guesses your wins took.
  • Which tutorials you have already seen.
  • Your settings — music, sound effects, vibration and chosen language (English, Arabic or French).
  • Your advertising choice, if you were asked for it (see section 5).
  • Counters used to keep advertisements infrequent and to limit reward videos per day.

The daily challenge is chosen from the current date on your device; the game does not contact any server to obtain it. The game also reads your screen's size and pixel density so the layout and the banner fit your screen, and your device's country setting (from the SIM card, the mobile network or the language settings) only to decide whether the advertising choice of section 5 must be shown. This happens on the device and nothing is sent anywhere by the game.

Sharing a result. After a daily challenge you may tap Share. The game then hands a short text (the puzzle number and a row of symbols, with no personal information) to Android's own share sheet, and you choose where it goes. We never receive it.

There is no account, no cloud save and no synchronisation between devices.

4. Advertising: Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads

MINDLOCK is free and supported by advertising. Advertisements are delivered by Unity LevelPlay, an advertising mediation service operated by Unity Technologies (which includes the former ironSource), and by the Unity Ads network. Both are software from Unity included in the app. LevelPlay chooses, for each request, which network provides the advertisement; more networks may be added in the future, in which case they will be listed here. Three formats are used:

  • Banner — a small advertisement at the bottom of the screen, in a space the game reserves for it so it never covers the board.
  • Interstitial — a full-screen advertisement between levels. None are shown during your first ten levels or during a tutorial; afterwards, at most one after every three levels you complete.
  • Rewarded — an advertisement you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward: free coins in the shop (a limited number per day), a power-up, doubled level coins, or extra attempts to continue a level. Nothing is taken from you if it fails to play, and you are never required to watch one.

We do not collect advertising data ourselves. The game does not read, store or transmit your advertising identifier, your IP address or any device identifier. It asks the advertising software to show an advertisement, and that software communicates with Unity and, through it, with the advertising networks and advertisers taking part.

According to Unity's own published disclosures for its advertising software, it collects and shares the following, encrypted in transit:

5. Consent and your advertising choices

If your device's country is in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows its own consent message the first time it starts:

  1. You choose Personalised ads or General ads only. Both choices are equally easy, and the game is the same whichever you pick.
  2. No advertisement is requested before you answer.
  3. Your answer is saved on your device (section 3) and passed to the advertising software, which then shows only non-personalised advertisements if you chose general ads.

Changing your mind. In those regions, Settings shows a Privacy choices button that reopens the same message so you can change your decision at any time; the new choice applies straight away.

Independently of the game, you can reset or delete your Android advertising ID, and turn off ad personalisation, in your device's own settings — see the Android help page linked in section 15. If you delete your advertising ID, you will still see advertisements, but they will not be personalised.

You can also play with the device offline. The game is fully playable without a network connection; when there is no connection, no advertisement can be requested.

6. Why information is processed

  • To run the game — saving your progress, items and settings on your device so you can continue where you stopped.
  • To suit your device — fitting the layout to your screen, on the device.
  • To fund the game — showing advertisements, which is how a free game without purchases is paid for.
  • To measure and protect advertising — Unity's measurement, fraud prevention and security purposes described in section 4.

We do not profile you, we do not build audiences, and we do not sell anything about you, because we do not receive anything about you.

7. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the processing described above rests on:

  • Consent — for personalised advertising and for storing or reading information on your device for advertising purposes. Consent is collected through the game's consent message and may be withdrawn as described in section 5.
  • Legitimate interests — for non-personalised advertising, for security and fraud prevention, and for keeping the game working correctly, balanced against your rights and freedoms.

Data stored only on your device is not transmitted to us and is under your control.

8. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell, rent or share information about you, because none reaches us. The third parties involved in the running of the game are Unity Technologies, through its advertising software (LevelPlay and Unity Ads) and the advertising partners it works with, and Google Play as the distribution platform.

Unity operates globally, so the information it processes for advertising may be transferred to and processed in countries other than yours, including the United States and Israel, under the safeguards described in Unity's own privacy policy. We have no separate transfer mechanism of our own because we transfer nothing.

9. Retention and deletion

On your device. Your saved progress and settings remain until you remove them. They are erased when you uninstall the game, or when you clear the app's data in Android Settings → Apps → MINDLOCK → Storage. Once erased, progress cannot be restored: there is no backup on our side.

With Unity. Information processed for advertising is retained by Unity under its own policies. Because we never receive it, we cannot delete it for you. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings, and contact Unity through the tools described in its privacy policy.

We hold no user database, so there is nothing for us to delete on request. If you write to us with a privacy question, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it.

10. Security

Saved game data is written to the app's private storage, which Android isolates from other applications. Communication performed by the advertising software is encrypted in transit using TLS.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Because the game holds no personal information and no account, the practical risk to you is limited to the loss of local game progress — for example if you uninstall the app.

MINDLOCK is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children, and it tells the advertising software that it is not child-directed.

Because we operate no servers and hold no user records:

If you want help exercising a right, write to aissamak121991@gmail.com and we will do what we can and point you to the correct provider where the data is not ours. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
