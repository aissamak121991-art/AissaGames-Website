Jelly Fuse — Merge Cubes

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026 · Last updated: November 8, 2026

The short version. Jelly Fuse is a single-player merge puzzle game. Your progress, coins, items and settings are saved only on your device. We have no servers and no accounts, so we never receive them. The current version contains no advertising software, no analytics and no in-app purchases. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

At a glance

  • No account, no sign-in, no profile.
  • No name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.
  • No analytics service, crash-reporting service or advertising software in the game.
  • Game progress stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.
  • Reminder notifications are created on your device, are optional, and can be turned off.
  • There are no in-app purchases.

1. Who we are

Jelly Fuse ("the game", "the app") is developed and published by ArdzGames ("we", "us", "our").

The Android application identifier is com.ardzgames.jellyfuse. The game is distributed through Google Play.

For any question about this policy, write to aissamak121991@gmail.com.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers the Jelly Fuse mobile game only. It does not cover Google Play or any other service you may reach from the game. Those are governed by their own policies.

3. Information stored on your device

The game saves a small record in the app's private storage on your device. The game does not upload it, and we never receive it. It contains no personal information:

  • Your progress: the level you have reached, the stars earned on each level, and the milestones you have achieved.
  • Your in-game items: coins, power-ups and the mascot's outfits.
  • Which tutorials and map areas you have already seen.
  • Your settings: music, sound effects, vibration, reduced effects and notifications.
  • Counters that limit how often offers appear each day.

There is no account, no cloud save and no synchronisation between devices run by us.

4. Notifications

The game can show reminder notifications. They are scheduled on your device from your saved progress, and no notification server is involved. On Android 13 and later, the system asks for your permission first. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in Android settings at any time, and the game works the same without them.

5. Sharing the game

If you tap Share, the game hands a short invitation and a link to the game's Google Play page to Android's own share sheet, or copies them to your clipboard. The text contains no personal information. You choose where it goes, and we never receive it.

6. Advertising

The current version contains no advertising software and shows no advertisements. It does not read your advertising identifier.

Advertising may be added in a future version. If it is, we will update this policy before that version is released, and it will name the advertising providers and describe the information they process.

7. Third parties

We do not sell, rent or share information about you, because none reaches us. The game is built with the Unity engine, and we have not enabled Unity's analytics or crash-reporting services.

Google Play distributes the game and handles its download and updates under Google's Privacy Policy. If you join a test of the game, Google Play manages that too.

8. Retention and deletion

Your saved progress and settings remain on your device until you remove them. You can erase them by uninstalling the game, or by clearing the app's data in Android Settings → Apps → Jelly Fuse → Storage. We keep no copy, so we cannot restore them for you.

We hold no user database, so there is nothing for us to delete on request. If you write to us, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it.

9. Android permissions

Permissions the app declares, and why
PermissionWhy it is there
INTERNET Declared by default in the game's engine build. The game sends no information of its own, and it plays without a connection.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Shows the optional reminder notifications described in section 4.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Declared by the game's notification component, so that scheduled reminders can be restored after your device restarts.
VIBRATE The short vibration feedback in the game, which you can turn off in Settings.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or media, telephone or SMS permissions.

10. Age of players and children's privacy

Jelly Fuse is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. The game does not ask for your age or date of birth.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, for example by email, contact us at aissamak121991@gmail.com and we will delete it.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Because we operate no servers and hold no user records, data on your device is under your direct control, and clearing the app's data removes it. For any question or help, write to aissamak121991@gmail.com.

12. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last updated. Significant changes, including the addition of advertising, are published here before the version they apply to is released.
