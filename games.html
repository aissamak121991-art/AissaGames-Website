2. How we make games

We build games that are quick to understand and rewarding to master. Our principles are simple:

  • Clear rules. You should be able to learn a game in a minute, and every rule should be written down.
  • Skill over luck. Wherever possible, results come from your own decisions.
  • Phone first. Our games are designed for portrait play, short sessions and one hand.
  • Many languages. We localise our games so more players can enjoy them in their own language.
  • Respect for players. Each game has its own privacy policy that explains the data it uses.

3. What comes next

We are an active studio and new games are in the works. When a new game is released, it will appear on this page with its own how-to-play guide, privacy policy and terms, kept separate from the other games so that each document only describes the game it belongs to.
