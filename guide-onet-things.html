How to play Onet Things

How to connect tiles, read the board and clear levels faster.

In short Onet Things is a match-and-connect puzzle. Find two identical tiles that can be joined by a path with at most two turns, tap them, and they disappear. Clear every tile to finish the level.

1. About Onet Things

Onet Things belongs to the family of connect puzzles known as Onet games. Instead of a single theme such as only fruit or only sweets, every board holds a varied mix of collectible objects, from everyday items and treats to gems, curios and lucky symbols. The variety is the point: each level feels different, and learning to recognise similar-looking pieces quickly is part of the challenge.

The game has 160 hand-built levels and supports 17 languages, including Arabic with right-to-left layout.

2. The goal

Every tile on the board has an identical twin. Your task is to remove all tiles by connecting twins, before the level timer runs out. Each successful connection removes two tiles and makes the board more open, which in turn makes new connections possible.

3. How tiles connect

Two matching tiles can be removed when you can draw a line between them that:

  • uses only horizontal and vertical segments,
  • has at most two turns (three segments), and
  • passes only through empty squares, never through another tile.

The path may run through the empty area around the board, so tiles on the edges are often easy to connect. If you tap two tiles that look identical but cannot be connected, nothing is removed. Check whether another tile is in the way or whether the path would need a third turn.

4. Reading the board

  • Start at the edges. Tiles on the outside have the clearest paths, and removing them opens the interior.
  • Look for straight lines. Two identical tiles in the same row or column with nothing between them always connect.
  • Watch for look-alikes. Many objects share colours or shapes. Compare details before tapping.
  • Think about what a pair unlocks. Prefer matches that open a path to other tiles over matches that change nothing.

5. Hints, shuffles and extra time

Three helpers are available when you are stuck:

  • Hint shows a pair that can be connected right now.
  • Shuffle rearranges the remaining tiles when no further matches are available.
  • Add time gives you extra seconds on the clock.

They are limited, so keep them for the levels where you really need them. Learning to find matches yourself is the best way to improve.

6. Levels and worlds

The 160 levels are arranged in themed worlds with their own backgrounds, and the boards grow larger and more complex as you progress. Early levels use smaller boards and fewer kinds of objects, so you can learn the rules. Later boards are bigger, with more different pieces, and they reward planning the order of your matches rather than tapping the first pair you see.

7. Tips for clearing boards

  1. Scan before you tap. Take a second to look at the whole board. The first pair you notice is not always the best one.
  2. Clear edges first. It creates empty lanes that make later matches easier.
  3. Keep an eye on the clock but do not panic. A calm plan beats rushed taps.
  4. Save a shuffle for late in the level, when few tiles remain and fewer matches are available.
  5. Learn the pieces. The more familiar you are with the objects, the faster you recognise twins.

8. Common questions

Why can I not connect two identical tiles?

Either another tile blocks the path, or the shortest path would need more than two turns. Remove tiles nearby first to open a lane.

Can I change the language?

The game offers 17 languages, including Arabic and French, and follows your device language where it is supported.

9. Privacy and terms
