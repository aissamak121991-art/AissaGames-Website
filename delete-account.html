Permanently remove your Unity Player Account and all associated Kharbga data. This action cannot be undone.

Deletion is permanent Deleting your Unity Player Account permanently erases your Kharbga progress and cannot be reversed. Once complete, your data cannot be recovered by you or by AissaGames.

What gets deleted

When you delete your Unity Player Account, the following data linked to Kharbga is permanently removed:

  • Authentication & identity — your Unity Player Account and linked sign-in methods (Google or email).
  • Cloud Save — all saved progress, settings, and statistics stored in the cloud.
  • Leaderboards — your scores and ranking entries are removed from all leaderboards.
  • Economy — your virtual currency and item balances are erased and forfeited.

Any local guest progress that was never linked to an account stays on your device and can be removed by uninstalling the game or clearing its data.

Before you delete

  • There is no way to recover your account or progress after deletion.
  • Virtual currency and items have no monetary value and are not refundable upon deletion.
  • If you only want to switch devices, you do not need to delete — signing in on the new device restores your Cloud Save instead.
  • Deletion is processed by Unity, our account provider, through its official deletion portal.

How to delete your account

  1. Note your sign-in method

    Remember whether you signed in to Kharbga with Google or email. You will use the same method to verify your identity in the deletion portal.

  2. Open the Unity deletion portal

    Use the button below to go to Unity's official Player Account deletion page at player-account.unity.com.

  3. Sign in and confirm

    Sign in with the account you use for Kharbga, then follow Unity's on-screen instructions to request permanent deletion.

  4. Deletion is processed

    Unity permanently deletes your account and the associated Cloud Save, Leaderboard, and Economy data. This cannot be undone.
