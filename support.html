Help & Support

Kharbga Support Center

Guides and answers for installing, signing in, saving progress, competing, and reporting issues. Can't find what you need? Contact us.

Installation & setup

Kharbga is available on supported mobile platforms. Open your device's app store, search for "Kharbga by AissaGames", and tap install. Once installed, launch the game — no account is required to start playing.

Kharbga is designed to run smoothly on modern phones and tablets. If the game runs slowly, close background apps, make sure your operating system and the game are up to date, and ensure you have a little free storage available.

Updates are delivered through your app store. Enable automatic updates, or open the store, go to your library, and update Kharbga manually. Keeping the game updated ensures the best performance and the newest features.

Sign-in & accounts

Open the game's sign-in screen and choose Continue with Google. Select the Google account you want to use and approve the request. Your Unity Player Account is then linked to Google, so your progress is protected and available on any device where you sign in.

Yes. Choose Continue with email on the sign-in screen and follow the steps to create or sign in to a Unity Player Account with your email address. Email and Google sign-in both give you cross-device Cloud Save.

A Unity Player Account is the secure identity that powers online features in Kharbga — Cloud Save, leaderboards, and your in-game economy. It is provided by Unity Gaming Services. You can read how we use it in our Privacy Policy.

A guest account lets you play immediately without signing in, but progress stays only on your device and can be lost if you uninstall the game or reset the device. We strongly recommend linking your guest account to Google or email so your progress is safely backed up.

Simply sign in again with the same method you used before (Google or email) on any device, and your progress will be restored from Cloud Save. If you have trouble accessing your sign-in method, contact us and we will help.

You can permanently delete your Unity Player Account and all associated data at any time. Follow the guide on our Delete Account page. Deletion is permanent and removes Cloud Save, leaderboard entries, and economy balances.

Progress, leaderboards & economy

When you are signed in, Kharbga automatically saves your progress and settings to Unity Cloud Save. Sign in with the same account on another device and your game continues right where you left off.

Install Kharbga on the new device, open the game, and sign in with the same Google or email account you used before. Your Cloud Save is downloaded automatically. You do not need to delete anything on the old device.

Leaderboards, powered by Unity Leaderboards, record your results so you can compare your ranking with other players. You must be signed in for your scores to appear. Results may be validated server-side to keep competition fair.

Kharbga may include virtual currency and items managed by Unity Economy. These are used only within the game, have no real-world monetary value, and cannot be exchanged for money. See our Terms of Service for details.

Kharbga ships with a clean, ad-light design. If advertising is ever introduced, we will update our Privacy Policy beforehand and, where required, ask for your consent.

Bug reporting & known issues

Email us at aissamak121991@gmail.com or use the contact form. Please include your device model, operating system version, the game version, and clear steps to reproduce the problem. Screenshots are very helpful.

First, restart the game and make sure it is updated to the latest version. If crashes continue, restart your device and free up some storage. Kharbga automatically sends anonymous crash diagnostics so we can fix issues quickly — and you can always email us with details.

We track and resolve issues in each update. If you experience a problem such as sign-in errors on a slow connection or a save not syncing immediately, please retry with a stable connection first, then contact us if it persists. We publish fixes regularly.

We are continuously improving Kharbga with new features, polish, and localisation (including Arabic and French). Keep automatic updates enabled to receive the latest version as soon as it is available.
