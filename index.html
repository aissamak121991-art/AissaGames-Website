Kharbga — the North African game of strategy

Kharbga is a centuries-old two-player abstract strategy game played across North Africa. AissaGames rebuilds it with a premium, faithful experience: authentic rules, an elegant board, and smooth play on every device.

  • Authentic 7×7 board and traditional capture rules.
  • Play against a friend or a sharpened AI opponent.
  • Cloud Save and leaderboards powered by Unity Gaming Services.
  • Free to play, with optional purchases and rewarded ads you choose to watch.
